We're a research group at [ICFO](https://www.icfo.eu/), Barcelona, led by Prof. Antonio Acín.

The main research objective of the group is to understand how quantum laws can be exploited to design novel protocols for information processing and communication, with an emphasis on quantum cryptography. The research effort goes from very abstract questions, such as security proofs of cryptographic protocols or characterising correlations beyond quantum physics, to proposals for the implementation of quantum information protocols and collaborations with experimental groups. The group activities also cover research questions in other fields, such as quantum thermodynamics, quantum foundations, quantum optics and many-body physics.

The group has opportunities for outstanding PhD students and postdocs. For more information, please contact Prof. Antonio Acín directly. Information on application procedures can be found on the ICFO jobs website.

See also the official [group page](https://www.icfo.eu/research-group/7/quantum-information/home/437/) on the ICFO site.
